About the Home

The Lakehouse is a stylish lakeside home, perfect for families or groups of up to 7 guests. The ground floor features an open-plan living area with a cosy lounge, large TV/DVD, and family-friendly games. The dining area is perfect for meals together, and the kitchen is fully equipped with oven, hob, microwave, fridge, freezer, and dishwasher. A utility room includes washing machine, tumble dryer, extra fridge, and separate WC. Free Wi-Fi is included.

Upstairs, there are three bedrooms: Master with king-size bed, lake views, smart TV/DVD, and en-suite shower room; Bedroom 2 with king plus single bed (convertible to three singles) and TV/DVD; Bedroom 3 with twin beds (zip-and-link to king) and TV/DVD. All bedrooms include hairdryers, and the family bathroom has a shower over the bath. A travel cot is available (please bring your own linen).

Location

The Lakehouse is set in the beautiful Cotswold Water Park near South Cerney, surrounded by lakes, walks, and watersports opportunities. Just a short drive from Cirencester, Cheltenham, and Bath, it’s ideal for exploring charming villages like Bourton-on-the-Water, Lechlade, and Stow-on-the-Wold. Cafés, pubs, and restaurants are within walking distance, and regular buses connect to nearby towns, making it perfect for relaxing or discovering the wider Cotswolds.
